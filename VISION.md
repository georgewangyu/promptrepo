# PromptRepo Vision

PromptRepo should be a curated library of reusable prompt layers and
agent-behavior overlays that are portable across AI tools.

## Product Thesis

The repo is useful when prompts become structured operating layers: sourced,
explained, copy-pasteable, and also available as skill-style files when the
behavior deserves orchestration.

## Goals

- Preserve dual-format entries: lightweight `prompts.md` plus structured
  `SKILL.md` where appropriate.
- Keep attribution and source context visible.
- Organize entries by real use case, not by vague inspiration.
- Make prompts actionable without requiring private context.

## Non-Goals

- Do not collect generic prompt snippets with no operating contract.
- Do not remove attribution when adapting third-party frameworks.
- Do not make entries depend on one private workflow or tool.
